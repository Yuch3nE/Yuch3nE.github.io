Effective / Last updated: September 23, 2026

中文 · English

DriveTrace 在你的设备上浏览、播放和导出行车录像，无需注册账号或登录 Tesla 账号。当前版本不会将你的录像、位置或行车数据上传给开发者。

1. 适用范围与开发者

本政策适用于由开发者 Yuch3nE 提供的 DriveTrace（行迹）桌面应用，以及本隐私政策网页。不同平台和版本的可用功能可能不同，下述处理仅在相应功能使用时发生。DriveTrace 是独立项目，并非 Tesla 官方应用。

2. 应用在本地处理哪些数据

  • 你选择的录像及关联文件：读取本地目录、移动存储设备中的录像、缩略图、事件元数据和 Track Mode 记录，用于建立资料库、搜索、播放、截图和导出。录像本身可能包含人脸、车牌或其他个人信息。
  • 位置和行车信息：当源文件包含这些内容时，读取拍摄时间、事件地点、经纬度、行驶轨迹、车速、转向、踏板状态等数据，用于事件展示、行车信息叠加和赛道回放。位置来自你选择的文件，应用不会为此主动获取设备的实时定位。
  • 本地使用状态与设置：保存来源目录和文件标识、资料库索引、收藏、最近浏览记录、界面与播放偏好、导出任务和输出位置。部分平台还会保存系统授予的目录访问授权，以便下次打开已选目录。
  • 缓存与诊断信息：生成缩略图和处理所需的临时文件；运行或启用诊断功能时，可能在本地记录错误、时间、性能、设备或媒体处理信息。诊断内容可能涉及文件路径等信息，不会由应用自动发送给开发者。

3. 数据用途与对外传输

上述数据用于提供录像管理、播放、行车信息展示、截图、视频导出、偏好保存和故障排查。当前版本没有开发者运营的账号服务、广告、行为分析或自动崩溃上报功能；开发者不会通过应用自动接收、出售或向广告商共享这些本地数据，也不会将其用于广告画像或模型训练。

导出视频和截图可能包含原始画面以及你选择叠加的时间、位置或行车信息。是否分享、分享给谁由你决定。若你选择云盘同步目录、网络磁盘或其他由第三方管理的位置，相关文件可能由该服务传输和保存，具体取决于你的配置与该服务的隐私政策。

4. 存储、保留与删除

资料库、设置、缓存和任务记录保存在设备的应用数据目录或你选择的数据目录中；便携版本可保存在程序目录内。导出视频和截图保存在相应输出目录。应用读取来源录像，导出另存为新文件。

本地数据会保留到你清理、重置或删除为止，部分缓存和临时文件会随应用运行被替换或清理。你可使用当前版本提供的存储管理、清理或重置功能，或在退出应用后删除对应的应用数据。清理索引、移除来源或重置设置不等于删除源录像、已导出视频或截图；这些文件以及系统备份、云端副本需另行管理。卸载应用也不保证删除所有数据目录和输出文件。

5. 权限与数据安全

应用需要访问你选择的录像位置，并向应用数据目录及输出目录写入数据；部分平台通过系统文件选择器和目录授权实现。你可以停止使用对应来源，或在系统支持时撤销访问权限。拒绝相应访问会影响读取或导出功能。

本地数据的保护依赖设备账户、文件权限和你启用的磁盘加密等措施。请妥善管理设备、移动存储和导出文件；应用不承诺对本地资料库或导出文件提供额外加密。

6. 网站、应用商店与主动反馈

本政策页面没有加入广告、访问统计脚本、第三方字体或跟踪 Cookie。页面由 GitHub Pages 托管；GitHub 会为安全目的记录访问者 IP 地址。有关托管服务如何处理信息，请参阅 GitHub 隐私声明及 GitHub Pages 说明。

通过应用商店下载、购买或更新应用时，商店和操作系统可能根据各自政策处理账户、交易或诊断信息。这些处理与 DriveTrace 在本地读取录像是不同的活动。

如果你主动向开发者提交问题、日志或截图，开发者会接触到你提交的内容及所用平台展示的账户信息，并将其用于沟通、排查和解决问题。请先移除不必要的人脸、车牌、位置、文件路径等信息，不要在公开问题中上传敏感录像。反馈记录的保留和删除还受所用平台的机制约束。

7. 你的选择与隐私请求

你可以决定是否选择文件、显示行车信息、导出或分享内容，并按第 4 节管理本地数据。开发者无法远程访问或替你删除仅保存在你设备上的文件。对于你主动提供给开发者的信息，可以通过下方渠道提出查阅、更正或删除请求；开发者会依据适用要求及实际可访问的数据处理。

8. 未成年人

DriveTrace 面向需要管理行车录像的用户，并非专门面向儿童的服务。未成年人应在监护人指导下使用，避免公开自己或他人的个人信息。如你认为向开发者提交的反馈涉及不应提供的未成年人信息，请联系开发者处理。

9. 政策更新与联系

当应用的数据处理方式发生变化时，本政策会相应更新，并在页首标明日期；需要额外告知或取得同意的情形，将按适用要求处理。

开发者：Yuch3nE。有关本政策或隐私请求，请通过 隐私政策仓库的问题反馈联系。该渠道为公开页面，请仅描述请求类型，不要提交敏感个人信息。

Privacy Policy — English

Effective / Last updated: September 23, 2026 · 中文

DriveTrace browses, plays and exports dashcam recordings on your device. No registration or Tesla account login is required. The current version does not upload your recordings, location or driving data to the developer.

1. Scope and developer

This policy applies to the DriveTrace (行迹) desktop application provided by developer Yuch3nE and to this privacy policy webpage. Available features may vary by platform and version; the processing described below occurs only when the corresponding features are used. DriveTrace is an independent project and is not an official Tesla application.

2. Data processed locally by the application

  • Recordings and associated files you select: The application reads recordings, thumbnails, event metadata and Track Mode records from local folders and removable storage to build the library and support search, playback, screenshots and export. Recordings may contain faces, license plates or other personal information.
  • Location and driving information: When available in the source files, the application reads recording times, event locations, latitude and longitude, routes, speed, steering, pedal states and similar data to display events, overlay driving information and replay track sessions. Location information comes from the files you select; the application does not actively obtain your device’s real-time location for these features.
  • Local usage state and preferences: The application saves source folders and file identifiers, library indexes, favorites, recently viewed items, interface and playback preferences, export tasks and output locations. On some platforms, it also retains system-granted folder access authorizations so that previously selected folders can be reopened.
  • Caches and diagnostic information: The application generates thumbnails and temporary files needed for processing. During operation or when diagnostic features are enabled, errors, timing, performance, device or media-processing information may be recorded locally. Diagnostic information may include file paths and similar details. The application does not automatically send it to the developer.

3. Purposes and external transfers

The data described above supports recording management, playback, driving information display, screenshots, video export, preference storage and troubleshooting. The current version has no developer-operated account service, advertising, behavioral analytics or automatic crash reporting. The developer does not automatically receive these local data through the application, sell them, share them with advertisers, or use them for advertising profiles or model training.

Exported videos and screenshots may contain the original images and any time, location or driving information you choose to overlay. You decide whether to share them and with whom. If you select a cloud-synced folder, network drive or another location managed by a third party, that service may transfer and store the files according to your configuration and its privacy policy.

4. Storage, retention and deletion

The library, preferences, caches and task records are stored in your device’s application data folder or a data folder you select. Portable versions may store them in the application folder. Exported videos and screenshots are saved in their respective output folders. The application reads source recordings and saves exports as new files.

Local data remain until you clear, reset or delete them; some caches and temporary files are replaced or cleared during operation. You can use the storage management, cleanup or reset features available in your version, or delete the relevant application data after closing the application. Clearing an index, removing a source or resetting preferences does not delete source recordings, exported videos or screenshots. Those files, system backups and cloud copies must be managed separately. Uninstalling the application does not guarantee removal of all data folders and output files.

5. Permissions and data security

The application needs access to the recording locations you select and permission to write to its data and output folders. Some platforms provide this access through system file pickers and folder authorizations. You can stop using a source or revoke access where the operating system supports doing so. Denying the relevant access affects reading or exporting files.

Protection of local data depends on measures such as device accounts, file permissions and any disk encryption you enable. Please safeguard your device, removable storage and exported files. The application does not promise additional encryption for local libraries or exported files.

6. Website, app stores and voluntary feedback

This policy page contains no added advertising, visitor analytics scripts, third-party fonts or tracking cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. For information about the hosting service’s processing, see the GitHub Privacy Statement and GitHub Pages documentation.

When you download, purchase or update the application through an app store, the store and operating system may process account, transaction or diagnostic information under their own policies. These activities are separate from DriveTrace reading recordings locally.

If you voluntarily submit a question, log or screenshot to the developer, the developer receives the submitted content and any account information displayed by the platform you use, and uses it to communicate, investigate and resolve the issue. Remove unnecessary faces, license plates, locations, file paths and similar information first, and do not upload sensitive recordings to public issues. Retention and deletion of feedback records also depend on the platform’s mechanisms.

7. Your choices and privacy requests

You decide whether to select files, display driving information, export or share content, and can manage local data as described in Section 4. The developer cannot remotely access or delete files stored only on your device. For information you voluntarily provide to the developer, you may request access, correction or deletion through the contact channel below. The developer will handle requests according to applicable requirements and the data actually accessible to them.

8. Minors

DriveTrace is intended for people who need to manage dashcam recordings and is not a service specifically directed at children. Minors should use it under a guardian’s guidance and avoid publicly disclosing their own or others’ personal information. If you believe feedback submitted to the developer contains information about a minor that should not have been provided, please contact the developer.

9. Policy updates and contact

This policy will be updated when the application’s data practices change, with the date shown at the top. Where additional notice or consent is required, it will be handled according to applicable requirements.

Developer: Yuch3nE. For questions about this policy or privacy requests, use the privacy policy repository’s issue tracker. This is a public channel: describe only the type of request and do not submit sensitive personal information.
